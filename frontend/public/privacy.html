1Who we are

AutoGMap (indev.eko.in/autogmap) is run by Eko Bharat Ventures Private Limited (“Eko”, “we”). It helps Eko’s team find and reach people who may run an SBI Customer Service Point. Only people approved by Eko’s administrator can use it.

2What we collect when you sign in

InformationWhere it comes fromWhy
Name and email addressGoogle (if you use Sign in with Google) or the form you fill in To know who you are and show the administrator who is asking for access
Whether Google has verified your emailGoogle So only real, verified work addresses can sign in

We ask Google only for the basic sign-in permissions (openid, email, profile). We do not ask for, and cannot see, your Gmail, Drive, Contacts, Calendar or any other Google data. We do not keep the token Google sends; we check it once and then use our own session.

3How we use it

  • To sign you in and keep you signed in.
  • To let the administrator approve, change or remove your access.
  • To keep an audit log of sign-ins and actions, for security.
  • To email the administrator when someone asks for access.
Signing in with Google proves you own the email address. It does not by itself give access: the administrator still approves every account.

4Sharing

We do not sell, rent or share your information with anyone outside Eko, and we never use it for advertising. It is seen only by Eko’s AutoGMap administrator. We may disclose it only if the law requires.

AutoGMap’s use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

5Cookies

AutoGMap sets one sign-in cookie that keeps you signed in. It cannot be read by page scripts and is not used for tracking or advertising. The page also remembers your light or dark theme in your browser.

6Where it is kept and for how long

Your account information is kept on Eko’s own server in India, for as long as you have access to AutoGMap. When access is removed, the account is switched off; you can ask for it to be deleted (see below). Sign-in records are kept for security for as long as Eko’s policies require.

7Your choices

  • Ask what we hold about you, or to correct or delete it.
  • Stop using Sign in with Google at any time from your Google Account’s third-party connections.

8Other information in AutoGMap

AutoGMap also holds business listings from Google Maps and the WhatsApp conversations of people who reply to Eko. That information is handled under Eko’s data policies and is not part of the Google sign-in described here.

9Changes and contact

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date. For any question or request, write to the AutoGMap administrator at mansi.kanchan.intern@eko.co.in.
